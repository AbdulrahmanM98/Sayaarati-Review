# Requirements and testing — portfolio notes

These notes summarize the final academic report rather than claim that this is an approved standalone business requirements document.

## Research
The report records 29 answers for its first five survey questions and 12 responses to its optional sixth question. The sample is small and should not be described as representative of an entire market.

## Functional requirements
The report groups requirements under three roles:

- **User:** register, log in/out, search for vehicles, read reviews, write reviews.
- **Admin:** edit/delete site content and manage site appearance.
- **Controller:** authenticate and manage vehicle listings.

The complete detailed “system shall” wording appears in the graduation report's chapter 3 (printed pages 28–32).

## Non-functional requirements
The report groups goals under usability, security, efficiency, scalability, and availability (printed pages 33–35). Describing a security requirement does not establish that the implemented prototype was audited or hardened.

## Models
The report contains use-case diagrams, DFDs, data dictionary, activity diagrams, sequence diagrams, a class diagram and ERD. Refer to [the diagram inventory](diagram-inventory.md) for each figure and page.

## Testing
The final report includes documented signup, login and car-form test cases (printed pages 80–82). The repository makes no claim that automated tests were run or that a production service is maintained.

## Individual contribution
The case-study author reports coordinating with the academic supervisor, gathering requirements and surveys, drafting functional and non-functional requirements, creating Use Case/DFD/ERD diagrams, and contributing testing and documentation. The website is a team deliverable.
